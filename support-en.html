Help and support

Updated: 9 November 2026

How do I time a shift?

On Today, tap Start shift, and End shift when you leave. Forgot? You can fix the start time while it runs, or add a shift by hand later.

How is overtime worked out?

By default: 8.6 regular hours a day for a 5 day week, the first 2 overtime hours at 125% and the rest at 150%. A night shift has a 7 hour day; Shabbat and holidays pay 150%, with overtime at 175% and 200%. You can change every number in Pay rules.

Why does net pay differ from my payslip?

Net pay here is an estimate. Your payslip uses data the app does not have, such as Form 101, credit points, pension and tax coordination. You can tune the percentages in Deductions and net.

Restoring a subscription

Settings, Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Moving to a new iPhone

Data lives on the device and comes back with your device’s iCloud backup. Exporting a CSV each month is a good habit too.
