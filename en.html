Privacy Policy

Updated: 9 November 2026

In short

Nishmarli does not collect, send or share personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your shifts, workplaces, hourly rates and settings are stored only on your device, in the app’s own file. The widget reads a short summary on the same device.

You can erase everything at any time: Settings, Delete all data, or delete the app.

Purchases

Nishmarli Pro is bought through Apple. We never see or store payment details. Apple tells the app whether your account has an active subscription.

Calendar

If you choose to add a shift to your calendar, Apple’s own calendar screen opens. The app never reads your events and gets no access to your calendar.

Exports

PDF reports and CSV files are made on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

Third parties

Nothing is shared with third parties, because we hold no data. There are no ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with its date.
