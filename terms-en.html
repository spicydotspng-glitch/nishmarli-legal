An estimate only

Nishmarli estimates pay from the hours you log, your hourly rate and the rules you choose. The result is not an official payslip and not payroll, tax or legal advice. Your employer’s payslip is what counts.

Net pay depends on Form 101, credit points, tax coordination and more, so it is always an estimate.
